Polynomial vs gradient boosting

Two different ways to predict Ontario demand. The polynomial is one fixed formula using only weather and calendar. Gradient boosting is a team of small decision trees that can also use recent demand. Here they are compared on the same hours, for the two things the site predicts: the next hour and the next 7 days.
