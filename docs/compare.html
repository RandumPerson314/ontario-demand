Model comparison

Six models predict Ontario's electricity demand, and the IESO (the grid operator) publishes its own forecast. They are compared here on the same hours, for the two things this site predicts: the next hour and the next 7 days. A "naive" guess (repeat an earlier hour) is included as a yardstick: a model that can't beat it isn't adding much.

Show on the graphs and in the live tables:

IESO forecast status

The IESO's forecast is saved every time the site updates, then scored against actual demand like the models. Its chart feed also keeps about five days of the IESO's own past projections, which are used immediately so there is something to compare from day one.

2. Live results so far

Every prediction is saved before its hour happens, then scored against the IESO's actual demand. These use real weather forecasts, so expect them to be a little worse than the backtest. Models are scored on exactly the same hours so the comparison is fair.
